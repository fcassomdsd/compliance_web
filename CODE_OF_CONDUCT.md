# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, safe, and inclusive experience for everyone.

Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to collaborate in good faith and treat others with professionalism and empathy.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and respectful language.
- Providing constructive feedback focused on ideas and code.
- Being open to differing viewpoints and technical approaches.
- Accepting feedback gracefully.
- Showing patience with newcomers and people learning the stack.
- Prioritizing the security and privacy impact of technical decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, inflammatory comments, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Repeatedly dismissing feedback in bad faith.
- Any behavior that a reasonable person would consider threatening or abusive.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including:

- Issues, pull/merge requests, and code reviews.
- Repository discussions and linked communication channels.
- Community events and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

- Open a private report to the maintainers through the repository hosting platform, or
- Contact the maintainers via the project support channel listed in the README.

When reporting, include as much context as possible (links, timestamps, screenshots, and usernames).

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take any action they deem appropriate for violations, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will aim to be fair, proportionate, and focused on maintaining a healthy community.

## Good-Faith Clause

We recognize that misunderstandings happen. Contributors are encouraged to assume good intent initially, seek clarification, and resolve conflict constructively.
